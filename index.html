The Sereen Difference

Grown, processed, and extracted under one roof.

Most Sceletium on the US market passes through two or three intermediaries before it reaches a formulator — brokers, rebottlers, generic Asian suppliers with opaque sourcing. Sereen is different by construction.

We own the farm. We own the processing. We own the extraction. Every kilogram is cultivated in South Africa — the native range of Sceletium tortuosum — and processed at our own facility to a standardised alkaloid profile, verified by HPLC on every batch.

The result is an ingredient with a traceable story, consistent specifications, and the documentation package your QA team actually needs.
